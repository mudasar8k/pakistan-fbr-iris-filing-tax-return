**Filing a tax return in Pakistan shouldn't feel like decoding a puzzle — so I built an open-source AI Skill to help.**

Every year, filing on FBR IRIS trips people up in the same places: a wealth statement that won't reconcile, bank transfers between your own accounts counted as "income," confusion between a bank payment success screen and an actual FBR CPR, and the temptation to just plug a cash figure to make the unreconciled amount zero.

I kept seeing friends and first-time filers struggle with exactly these steps, so I decided to open-source what I'd learned as a structured, privacy-first AI Skill — something anyone can use, inspect, and improve.

**What it helps you navigate:**
- Salaried, freelancer and foreign-employer income
- Bank transaction classification (own transfers, reimbursements, pass-through funds)
- Wealth statement reconciliation — accurately, never by manufacturing a figure
- Withholding vs final/advance tax
- PSID payments and CPR verification
- Step-by-step guidance when you share IRIS screenshots

**What it deliberately does NOT do:** it does not file your taxes for you, invent numbers, or guarantee a correct return. It guides you, flags year-specific rules for professional verification, and never asks for passwords, OTPs or full CNIC/bank details. It's an unofficial educational tool — not affiliated with FBR — and professional advice still matters.

It ships with automated validation (GitHub Actions), fictional worked examples, and a privacy checklist.
