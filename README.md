# Pakistan FBR IRIS Filing Assistant

A community-oriented ChatGPT Skill for safely guiding individuals through Pakistan's FBR IRIS income-tax filing and wealth reconciliation. Includes tax-payment and CPR verification workflow.

**Unofficial; not affiliated with FBR. Not legal or tax advice.** Tax rules, menu labels and forms change. Verify the applicable tax-year law and confirm uncertain cases with a Pakistani tax professional.

## What it does

- Helps users review IRIS screenshots one step at a time.
- Tracks salary, bank profit, withholding, and tax computation without double-counting.
- Reconciles prior- and current-year assets, bank accounts, cash, gifts and liabilities.
- Differentiates own-account transfers, reimbursed expenses and pass-through funds from actual earnings.
- Explains PSID payments, CPR recognition and how to verify actual return submission.

**Critical safeguard:** Never manufacture a cash balance, expense or gift just to produce a zero unreconciled figure.
