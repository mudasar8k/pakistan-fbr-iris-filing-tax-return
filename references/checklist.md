# Accuracy and privacy checklist

## Before treating calculations as final
- [ ] Correct tax year, dates, residence and employment characterization.
- [ ] Each bank statement's opening and closing balance checked; accounts not omitted or double-counted.
- [ ] Physical cash is not copied blindly from last year and is supported by a reasonable reconstruction.
- [ ] Transfers between own accounts excluded from income.
- [ ] Incoming third-party transactions categorized using corroborating evidence; forwarding funds is not by itself earnings.
- [ ] Employer statements, IRIS prefilled data, and profit/withholding figures reconciled, including missing months.
- [ ] Personal expenses reflect realistic actual outflows, not an artificial amount chosen for zero reconciliation.
- [ ] Spouse/family loans have contemporaneous repayment obligation if listed as liabilities; gifts/support dated properly.
- [ ] Previous-year omissions handled transparently; any revision requirement checked.
- [ ] All IRIS errors/uncertainties explained to taxpayer and reviewed before submission.

## Payments and completion
- [ ] PSID belongs to correct taxpayer, tax year and tax type.
- [ ] Payment-provider success distinguished from FBR CPR; avoid duplicate payments.
- [ ] Return and wealth statement appear in Completed Tasks after submission; acknowledgement saved.

## Before publishing any case or screenshot
- [ ] Remove all names, NTN/CNIC, IBAN, account numbers, reference IDs, screenshots, transaction identifiers, contact info and precise addresses.
- [ ] Do not commit original bank statements, tax PDFs, email confirmations, exported IRIS data, or local backups.
- [ ] Use wholly fictional numbers and a clearly labelled demonstration if publishing an example.
- [ ] Never publish login details, session URLs or PSIDs.
