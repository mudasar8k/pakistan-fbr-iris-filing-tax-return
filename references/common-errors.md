# Common filing errors

> Not tax advice. Use alongside the [accuracy & privacy checklist](checklist.md).

## Reconciliation & wealth

- **Plugging cash in hand** to force a zero unreconciled amount. Never do this — reconstruct cash instead.
- Inventing a **gift, loan or expense** to balance wealth.
- Copying **last year's cash** figure without reconstruction.
- Treating **bank balance** as cash in hand (or vice versa).
- **Double-counting** by keeping cash+bank combined from a prior year and also listing them separately now.

## Income

- Missing **bank profit** income or its withholding.
- Omitting a **second employer** after a mid-year job change.
- Counting employer-deducted tax **twice**.
- Classifying **own-account transfers** or **reimbursements** as income.
- Assuming an **export/final-tax concession** applies without verifying eligibility.

## Withholding / advance tax

- Mixing **adjustable** and **final/minimum** tax items.
- Deriving withholding from a **guessed** base instead of the certificate.

## Payment & submission

- Believing a **bank success screen** equals an FBR **CPR**.
- Making a **second payment** when a CPR is merely delayed.
- Assuming **payment = filing** — forgetting to actually submit.
- Not confirming **both** the return and wealth statement reached **Completed Tasks**.

## Privacy

- Sharing **full CNIC/NTN, IBAN, OTP or passwords** with any assistant.
- Committing **real statements, receipts or screenshots** to a public repository.
