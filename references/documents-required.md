# Getting started & documents required

> Rules, thresholds and procedures change each tax year. Confirm details against the [official FBR sources](fbr-official-sources.md) and your live IRIS screen. This is not tax advice.

## Before you start

- An active **IRIS account** (registered on NTN/CNIC). Register only through official FBR channels.
- The correct **tax year** (normally 1 July–30 June).
- Knowledge of your **residency status** for the year (resident vs non-resident), which affects scope of income and whether a wealth statement is required.

## Documents to gather

**Income**
- Salary certificate(s) from each employer (Pakistan and foreign), showing gross salary, allowances/perquisites and tax deducted.
- Freelance/business receipts, invoices, and platform/payment-gateway statements.
- Foreign income records and any foreign tax paid.
- Bank **profit/interest** certificates and tax deducted on profit.

**Banking**
- Full-year **bank statements** for every account (opening and closing balances on correct dates).
- Records distinguishing transfers between your own accounts.

**Withholding / advance tax**
- Telecom/mobile tax certificates.
- Tax deducted on cash withdrawals, vehicle, property, utility and other transactions.
- Any advance tax paid directly.

**Assets & liabilities (wealth statement)**
- Immovable property documents with acquisition cost/valuation.
- Vehicle registration and cost.
- Investments, savings, prize bonds, etc.
- Loans receivable/payable with supporting terms.
- Prior-year wealth statement (for opening balances).

**Payment**
- PSID / CPR records once tax is paid.

## Privacy reminder

Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, full CNIC/NTN or IBANs with any assistant. Provide **redacted** documents and share only the pages needed for a specific step.
