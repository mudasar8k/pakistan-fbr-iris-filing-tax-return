# Official FBR references (reviewed 24 October 2026)

Links are references to check when providing updated filing guidance; do not assume screenshots or rates stay unchanged.

- [File Income Tax Return](https://fbr.gov.pk/categ/file-income-tax-return/51147/80860/71160) — confirms return plus wealth statement, reconciliation requirement, successful completion via both forms moving from Draft to Completed Tasks.
- [Pay Income Tax](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/categ/pay-income-tax/51147/40850/81158) — FBR says Computerized Payment Receipt (CPR) normally reflects in IRIS within 24 hours of payment.
- [FBR ePayment 2.0 announcement](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/fbr-introduces-new-payment-creation-system/174167) — PSID and CPR/payment flow.
- [Revising Returns and Wealth Statements](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/categ/file-income-tax-return/51147/80860/71161) — check eligibility and current procedural requirements before suggesting a prior-year correction.
- [IRIS knowledge base](https://help.fbr.gov.pk/?p=8777) — declaration and task-status workflow.
- [IRIS portal](https://iris.fbr.gov.pk/) — official filing interface.

Important: quoted field codes, statutory rates, filing deadlines, prescribed thresholds and resident/nonresident rules are tax-year-specific. Verify them against current FBR law and the user's actual IRIS interface; do not freeze anecdotal numbers into this skill.
