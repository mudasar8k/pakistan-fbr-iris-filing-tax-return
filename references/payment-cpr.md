# Tax payment & CPR verification

> Not tax advice. Verify the current payment flow on the [official FBR sources](fbr-official-sources.md).

## The flow

1. **Read the computation** (Tax Chargeable, Withholding Income Tax, Admitted Income Tax). Treat the amount as **provisional** until your records are checked.
2. **Generate a PSID** (Payment Slip ID) from the official IRIS / ePayment interface for the correct **taxpayer, tax year and tax type**.
3. **Pay** through an authorized channel (bank, ADC/app, etc.) using that PSID.
4. **Verify the CPR** (Computerized Payment Receipt) — this is the proof FBR has recorded your payment.

## Payment success ≠ CPR

A bank or app **success screen** proves the payment went through *that provider*. It does **not** by itself confirm FBR has linked it to your return. Always verify the **CPR and its tax-year/tax-type linkage** separately.

## If no CPR appears

- FBR states a CPR generally reflects in IRIS **within about 24 hours**.
- If it hasn't appeared, check the payment section and the official **PSID/CPR search**, or contact FBR support.
- **Do not make a second payment** just because the computation still shows the assessed tax — that commonly causes double payment.

## Payment ≠ filing

Paying tax is **not** the same as filing the return. After payment you must still **submit** the return and wealth statement and confirm both are in **Completed Tasks**.
