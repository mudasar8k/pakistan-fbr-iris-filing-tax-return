# Salaried taxpayers

> Not tax advice. Rates, slabs and field codes are tax-year-specific — verify against [official FBR sources](fbr-official-sources.md) and your live IRIS screen.

## Workflow

1. **Select tax year** and open the Normal Return.
2. **Enter employment income** under the Employment / Salary category:
   - Gross salary, allowances and perquisites as shown on your salary certificate.
   - Enter tax **actually deducted** by the employer (from the certificate), as an adjustable credit.
3. **Add other income** (e.g. bank profit) in its correct category — do not merge it into salary.
4. **Withholding/advance tax:** record other deductions (telecom, cash withdrawal, vehicle, property) with certificates. Keep **adjustable** credits separate from **final/minimum** tax items.
5. **Review the computation table**: taxable income → tax chargeable → credits/payments → admitted tax.
6. **Wealth statement:** complete assets, liabilities, cash and reconciliation (see [wealth statement](wealth-statement.md)).
7. **Pay** any admitted tax via PSID and **verify the CPR** (see [payment & CPR](payment-cpr.md)).
8. **Submit** and confirm both forms are in Completed Tasks.

## Common pitfalls

- Entering the **taxable** figure where **gross** is required, or vice versa — follow the on-screen field labels.
- Counting employer-deducted tax twice (once as salary data, once as a manual credit).
- Forgetting bank-profit income or its withholding.
- Treating own-account transfers as income (see [bank reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)).

## Multiple employers / mid-year change

If you changed jobs, combine certificates from **all** employers for the year and sum both the income and the tax deducted. Keep each certificate as evidence.
