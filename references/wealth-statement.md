# Wealth statement & reconciliation

> Not tax advice. Field codes and valuation rules are year-specific — verify against [official FBR sources](fbr-official-sources.md).

## Principle

The wealth statement explains the **change in your net wealth** during the year. The goal is an **accurate** picture, not a forced zero.

**Wealth equation:**
```
closing net wealth − opening net wealth
   = recognized inflows − outflows + legitimate documented adjustments
```

An **Unreconciled Amount = 0** means the arithmetic balances — it does **not** prove that cash, income or expenses were independently substantiated.

## Steps

1. **Opening balances:** carry forward *actual* prior-year assets and liabilities. If the prior year had omissions, flag them transparently (see revision rules) — do not silently alter the prior year.
2. **Assets:** record each asset in its correct field:
   - Immovable property — by the legally required valuation field (acquisition cost vs other).
   - Vehicles, investments, prize bonds, personal possessions.
   - Bank balances (closing, per statements) — **separate from cash**.
   - **Cash in hand** — reconstructed, not copied (see below).
3. **Liabilities:** only **genuine** loans with a repayment obligation. Match each liability to its inflow.
4. **Gifts/loans:** classify family transfers correctly (see below).
5. **Expenses:** enter realistic actual outflows by category; do not plug the unreconciled difference.
6. **Review** the reconciliation and explain the sign and source of any mismatch.

## Cash in hand (critical)

Reconstruct, never guess:
```
opening cash + actual cash receipts + cash withdrawals
   − cash deposited − actual cash spending = closing cash
```
**Never change cash in hand merely to make the unreconciled amount zero.** If it won't reconcile, investigate missing income, transfers or expenses instead.

## Gifts, family transfers and loans

- A transfer is a **loan** only if there is a genuine repayment obligation with contemporaneous terms. Voluntary repayment alone does not make it a loan.
- A **gift** typically needs a supporting instrument/evidence trail; confirm its date falls in the tax year.
- Match the inflow to the correct category before adding it.

## Pass-through & reimbursements

Money that merely passed through your account (reimbursements, funds forwarded for someone else) is **not** your income or expense. Match incoming and outgoing by date, amount and payee, and treat any retained fee separately.
