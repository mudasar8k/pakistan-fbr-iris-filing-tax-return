# Withholding & advance tax

> Not tax advice. Which deductions are adjustable vs final, and their rates, are year-specific — verify against [official FBR sources](fbr-official-sources.md).

## Adjustable vs final — get this right

- **Adjustable** tax is credited against your total tax liability for the year (e.g. tax deducted on salary, many advance-tax items).
- **Final / minimum** tax discharges the liability for that income and is **not** a general credit against other income.

Putting a final-tax item into the adjustable column (or vice versa) distorts the computation. When unsure, verify the category for that specific item and year.

## Common deduction sources

- **Salary** — tax deducted by employer.
- **Bank profit/interest** — tax on profit.
- **Telecom/mobile** — advance tax on bills/top-ups.
- **Cash withdrawals** — where applicable.
- **Vehicle** — registration/token/transfer.
- **Property** — purchase/sale/rent, where applicable.
- **Utilities** and others as prescribed.

## How to handle them

1. Collect the **certificate/evidence** for each deduction.
2. Never derive a withholding amount from a **guessed** taxable base — if you must estimate, label it an estimate and verify the certificate.
3. Enter each item in its correct IRIS field and category.
4. Reconcile the total against your records before accepting the computation.

## Advance tax paid directly

If you paid advance tax yourself (e.g. instalments), record it with the challan/CPR so it is credited correctly.
